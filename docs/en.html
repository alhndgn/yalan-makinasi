Privacy Policy

This policy explains what data the Lie Machine app (Android and web) processes, where it is stored, and why it is never shared.

Last updated: 18 November 2026
In short: everything you write — including the names you mention — stays with you. No account is required. No data is used for advertising, marketing or profiling, and nothing is sold, rented or transferred to third parties.

1. Data controller

Lie Machine is built by Teq Studio. Contact: teqstudio@hotmail.com

2. What we process

DataPurposeWhere
Your choices (who for, what happened, tone, channel) and the generated excuse texts Generating excuses, the risk indicator and the question rehearsal App database in server mode; device-local storage in the APK
The name of the person you lied to (e.g. "Sarah") Grouping records by person and remembering history for the next suggestion App database / device storage — only you can see it
Optional person profile (relationship, gender, closeness, notes) Choosing how the AI addresses that person App database / device storage
Random device id (UUID) Keeping records separate per device — there are no accounts App database (generated on device; it is not an advertising id)

We never ask for your name, e-mail, phone number, location, contacts, photos or address book.

3. Where data is stored

  • Android app: only on your device. Removing the app or clearing its data deletes everything.
  • Web version: in the app's own database (SQLite) on its server, used for nothing else.

4. AI processing

Texts are generated by a language model. During generation only your chosen context (who for, what happened, tone, channel, the names you typed and previous entries for that person) is sent to the model service. Your name, e-mail, device id and location are never sent. The provider processes this data only to produce the answer. If the AI is unreachable, the app falls back to an on-device template engine and no data leaves the device.

5. Sharing

Your data is never shared with any company, institution or person; it is not passed to ad networks, data brokers or analytics services and is never sold. The only exceptions:

  • Actions you start yourself: using "Copy", WhatsApp or the share sheet sends the text to the app you picked.
  • Legal obligations: a valid court order or statutory requirement.

6. Retention and deletion

Records stay until you delete them. You can delete each entry in the ledger, and clearing app data removes everything. To have server-side records deleted, e-mail teqstudio@hotmail.com.

7. Permissions

The app only requests internet access. It does not request camera, microphone, location, contacts or storage permissions. The share-card feature creates an image and saves it to your downloads folder — on device only.

8. Ads

This version contains no ads and does not collect an advertising id. If ads are added later, only Google AdMob will be used; the advertising id and device information would be shared with Google for ad delivery, and this policy plus the store data-safety declaration would be updated in advance.

9. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from them.

10. Purpose and limits

The app is for entertainment. It does not produce official documents, medical reports, signatures, invoices, or court/police content; the generation engine refuses such requests.

11. Your rights (KVKK / GDPR)

You may access, correct or delete your data and object to processing. Write to teqstudio@hotmail.com; we answer within 30 days.

12. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is published here with an updated date. Significant changes are announced inside the app.
